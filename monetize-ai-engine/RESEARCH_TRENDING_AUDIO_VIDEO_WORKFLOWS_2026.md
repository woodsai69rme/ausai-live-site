## 1. 🎙️ Breakthrough Audio & TTS Models (2026)

### A. Kokoro-82M (The New Standard in Open-Source TTS)
- **Architecture:** Compact 82 Million parameter model running on ordinary CPUs (sub-50ms latency) without requiring a discrete GPU.
- **Quality:** Rivals ElevenLabs in natural rhythm, breathing pauses, and cadence with **$0 API costs**.
- **Use Case in Micro-SaaS:** Perfect for embedding directly into local video agents, live voice chatbots, and automated audiobook generation.

### B. F5-TTS (Zero-Shot Voice Cloning)
- **Capabilities:** High-fidelity zero-shot voice cloning from a 5-second audio sample.
- **Multilingual Support:** Auto-translates and clones creator voices across English, Spanish, German, French, and Japanese for international YouTube syndication.

## 2. 🎥 Open-Weights Video Generation: Wan 2.2 / Wan 2.6
- **Architecture:** State-of-the-art cinematic video generation model with deep prompt adherence and realistic physics.
- **ComfyUI Integration:** Runs efficiently via quantized GGUF / low-VRAM flags (`--lowvram --force-fp16`), generating 8-to-16 frame video batches on 8GB-16GB GPUs.
- **Micro-SaaS Product:** Turnkey video asset generation for faceless TikTok/Reels marketing and B-roll automation.

## 3. 🤖 n8n AI Agents vs. Code-Native Swarms
- **Visual Webhook Layer (n8n)**: Best for non-technical users to trigger inbound Stripe webhooks, Discord alerts, and email parsing.
- **Code-Native Swarms (FastAPI + LangGraph / Python)**: Best for complex multi-step reasoning, AST code rewriting, and sandboxed Playwright browser execution.
- **The Ideal Hybrid Stack:**
  ```
  [Inbound Webhook / Stripe] ──► [n8n Visual Trigger] ──► [AutoMonetize AI Swarm] ──► [Kokoro/Edge-TTS + Wan 2.2 Video]
  ```
