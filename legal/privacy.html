Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

At AusAI Tech, we take data privacy and security seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and business information.

2. Data Collection

We only collect the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Contact details (Name, Email, Phone) provided via our contact forms or Calendly bookings.
  • Billing information (processed securely via Stripe; we never store full credit card details).
  • Project documentation, API keys, and business data shared strictly for the purpose of executing a Statement of Work.

3. AI and Model Training (CRITICAL)

We absolutely never use client data to train, fine-tune, or improve our own AI models or public models. Any data processed through third-party APIs (like OpenAI or Anthropic) during our development process is strictly governed by their enterprise zero-retention policies, which we enforce by default.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To communicate regarding your project, support requests, or audit bookings.
  • To process payments and issue invoices.
  • To build and deploy the specific AI solutions outlined in your Statement of Work.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain project-related data only for the duration of the engagement and the subsequent warranty/support period. Upon termination of the project, all sensitive data (including API keys, proprietary documents, and database dumps) is securely deleted from our local systems unless otherwise requested by you.

6. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our business, including Stripe (payments), Calendly (scheduling), and Vercel (hosting). These providers have their own strict privacy policies and GDPR compliance frameworks.
