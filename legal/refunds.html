Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. 30-Day Pre-Kickoff Refund Policy

If you purchase a fixed-price package via our website (e.g., AI Chatbot Setup, n8n Automation Bundle) and change your mind before any work has commenced, you are entitled to a full 100% refund within 30 days of purchase. Simply reply to your receipt email to request the cancellation.

2. Non-Refundable Custom Builds

Because our services involve non-recoverable labor and time, refunds are not provided once the project kickoff has occurred or development work has begun. All custom projects require a 50% upfront deposit, which is strictly non-refundable to cover initial architectural planning, setup, and resource allocation.

3. 14-Day Delivery Warranty

We guarantee our work. While we do not offer refunds on completed projects, every project comes with a 14-day warranty following final delivery. If the delivered system fails to meet the specific acceptance criteria outlined in our mutually signed Statement of Work, we will fix the issue within 48 hours at zero additional cost to you.

4. Subscription / Retainer Cancellations

If you are on an ongoing "AI Partner" monthly retainer, you may cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice. You will be billed for any final period falling within that 30-day window, after which no further charges will apply. We do not provide prorated refunds for mid-month cancellations.
